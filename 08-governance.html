The order is the argument. The calibration was fitted, then held out, then run on a fresh confirmation — so the confirmation’s coverage is not the coverage the calibration was tuned to.

8.2 G18-A: checking the method against its sources

Unusual enough to call out. Before integration, the implementation was checked against the published sources it claims to follow — three crosswalks closed across 13 blocking checks, with page- and line-level registers shipping:

  • v4-andreadis-file-line-crosswalk-v1.csv
  • v4-molenaar-page-crosswalk-v1.csv
  • v4-rueschendorf-page-crosswalk-v1.csv

That is what makes the Andreadis (2017) predecessor credit in claim CL-03 a finding rather than a courtesy.

8.3 The claim-eligibility register

v4-phase18-claim-eligibility-v1.csv, 14 claims, frozen at the source-review gate. It governs the article’s wording: what may be asserted, what must be attributed, and what is out of bounds.

It ships as Table H1, and verify_semantics.R asserts all 14 are transcribed. A results table you can regenerate; a claim register you can only preserve.

8.4 The SWMDK lock

The Phase 17 protocol recorded the data object’s digest, version, dimensions and score support before the application ran. Since no responses ship, that lock is what makes the application’s numbers checkable at all — read_swmdk() asserts it on every load.

The digest appears in provenance/excluded-digests.csv with a reason: it is the digest of an R object, not a file, so it resolves to a live check rather than to something on disk.

8.5 Two documented departures from byte-identity

What Register
21 reference-code files bannered reference-code/ARCHIVAL-DIGESTS.csv
— including ordinal-h.R, with its defect notice same
10 authority records redacted provenance/redactions.csv

No code line was changed by the banners. The redactions touched two fields of the authority records: the one naming the command-line harness through which the author’s instruction arrived, and the one that held that instruction verbatim as a short colloquial message. The second is now an English authority token of the same form the companion package uses.

Neither field carried evidence. What each authority actually licensed is in authorized_scope, what it withheld is in explicitly_not_authorized, where it had to stop is in required_stop, and when it was recorded and what it descends from are in recorded_at_utc and parent_governance.sha256. Every one of those is unchanged, and they are what the gate reads.

verify_governance.R accepts the pre-modification digest from either register, which is what lets both departures coexist with a chain that closes.

The rule: a package may modify what it ships only if it records what the file was before.
