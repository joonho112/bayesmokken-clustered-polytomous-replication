7  The SWMDK application

639 pupils in 30 classrooms, cluster sizes 5–29, responses on 1–5.

Two prespecified scales: teacher (Items 1–6) and classmate (Items 8, 9, 10, 11, 13).

7.1 The result

7.1.1 Teacher scale

Respondent-weighted Equal-cluster
H 0.6199 0.6223
95 % CI [0.5728, 0.6692] [0.5743, 0.6698]
length 0.0964 0.0956

7.1.2 Classmate scale

Respondent-weighted Equal-cluster
H 0.5923 0.5829
95 % CI [0.5431, 0.6408] [0.5347, 0.6345]
length 0.0978 0.0998

The targets differ by +0.0024 on teacher and −0.0094 on classmate — in opposite directions. That is the informative-cluster-size effect showing up as something other than noise, and it is why the paper reports both rather than picking one.

7.2 What the clustering costs

Teacher scale Length
raw two-stage HBB (uncalibrated) 0.1327
calibrated HBB 0.0964
two-level delta comparator 0.1004
iid respondent bootstrap (ignores clustering) 0.0803

The iid interval is 20 % narrower than the calibrated cluster-aware one. That gap is the cost of taking the clustering seriously — an analyst who ignores it reports more precision than the design supports.

The calibrated interval reaches 96.0 % (teacher) and 98.5 % (classmate) of the delta comparator’s length, so the gain is real but modest against a well-specified analytic alternative.

7.3 Computation

12 tasks × 99,999 draws = 1,199,988 draws, zero invalid. The width multiplier at G = 30 is 0.7261.

Exact knots: 2 on the teacher respondent-weighted lane, 0 on equal-cluster. Those are diagnostic disclosures of the transport path, not error flags — and they bear on the archival defect, where an exact zero is the handled case.

7.4 Stability

Maximum split-half discrepancy 8.75e-4, against an interval about 0.096 wide. Minimum category mass 0.0031, minimum category count 2.

7.5 Independent reproduction

The oracle lane recomputes both scales from data(SWMDK) with the mokken package and reproduces Koopman et al. (2022)’s published values:

Scale Published Recomputed
teacher 0.62 0.6199
classmate 0.592 0.5923 
Rscript verification/oracles/run_oracles.R

This is the strongest external check in the package: a different implementation, run on the same data, reproducing numbers published by different authors.
